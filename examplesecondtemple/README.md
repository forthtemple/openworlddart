# Second Temple

Repository and support for *Second Temple* game. Demonstrates flutter based openworld game engine for iOS, macos, Android, Linux, Windows and Web.

Journey through the Second Temple in Jerusalem in 65 AD as a priest, pilgrim or shopkeeper, or roam as you please.
Gameplay Overview:

 - As a new priest you wake up in the Chamber of the Hearth with the other priests at the Second Temple. A friend is there to show you the Mikveh, the lotteries,  rituals like the Tamid and Ketoret (incense offering).

 - As a pilgrim, wake up at your home in Bethphage on the Mount of Olives and ride your donkey to the Temple for the Tamid

 - As a new mother seek purification after childbirth visiting the pool of Bethesda and making an offering at the Temple as described in Leviticus 12

 - As a shopkeeper, wake up with your husband and set up shop along the bustling Pilgrimage Road

 - Or, explore freely at your own pace visiting the Pool of Siloam, witnessing Jerusalem lit up at night during Sukkot or visiting the Western wall of the Temple Mount.
